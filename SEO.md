# SEO keywords

What people type when they want a journal. Use this when you write an article or a situation page.

The product stays in [`WHO-WE-ARE.md`](WHO-WE-ARE.md). This file does not change the loop, the promises, or the voice. It names the query the page should match.

Source: Serpstat, US Google, 25 Aug 2026. Market demand. Not our Search Console log. Refresh when GSC is connected or volume is pulled again.

---

## How to write from this

One page, one primary query.

- Put the primary query in the H1, the document title, and the slug if the slug is still free.
- Use close variants in the first two paragraphs.
- Title shape: `Journaling for anxiety | Journal42`
- Stop there. Do not list every related term. Do not write for density.

Ad language (Slack still open, kids asleep, the driveway, the review) belongs on ads and the homepage. It is not a search query. Do not force it into an H1.

## Not search. Ads.

These are real nights. People do not type them.

- ChatGPT as a journal (~50 / mo for `chatgpt journaling`). They already have the tab. `/chatgpt-as-a-journal` can exist. Do not treat it as an SEO bet.
- Tech workers, Slack still open, kids asleep, the driveway, the review. Homepage and ads.
