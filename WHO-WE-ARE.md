## What it is

Journal42 is a journaling app.

You write a journal entry. You get a short AI reflection you can answer.

The journal is the product. AI is a read after the writing. 

The user is getting the thought onto the page.

---

## What happens

1. **Write a journal entry.** Half-thoughts are enough. Start before you know what you are trying to say.
2. **Get a reflection.** It names what sat under the noise. Reply if you want to go further.
3. **It remembers.** Come back when you want the next one.

That loop is the product. How long we ask for, who we name, See `VARIATIONS.md`.

## Craft

How we write, on every surface. Not who we write for.

- Short sentences. Concrete rooms when a scene is needed.
- Direct. Warm without being soft. Do not cheerlead. Do not therapize. Do not sell the feeling of being seen.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or a rewritten sentence.
- No "X is Y, not Z." Do not define the product by what it is not. Say what happens.
- Care as its own sentences. Not as a contrast.

These also live in `website/.cursor/rules/no-em-dashes.mdc`.

Bad: `Journal42 is a journal, not a chatbot.`

Good: `Journal42 is a private journal. You write a fragment.`

### Product words


| Use                                                | Why                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| fragment, half-thought, unfinished thing, the loop | How people actually write                                   |
| reflection                                         | The AI output. A second read. Not a chat, insight, or coach |
| private page, your page                            | Where the writing lives                                     |




### Words we never use

- Chatbot, copilot, therapist, coach
- JovianX, anywhere except Privacy, Terms, and Contact
- Urgency that is not the night itself ("limited time," "don't miss")
- Consent or privacy UI as a marketing funnel

What we sound like for a given vertical (wellness, productivity, the ChatGPT tab) is a bet. Put it in `VARIATIONS.md`.
